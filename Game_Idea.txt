Game name: soon

You take the role of a security guard who works in a museum during the night, but later he discovers that some exhibits come to life when they are not being watched.

The goal is to survive the night without being caught by ghosts, otherwise you'll become one. 

The main mechanic is the player's flashlight, cause ghosts are unable to move when the flashlight is pointed at them. During the nights different ghost types will appear, like some will move faster than others or will be stronger against the flashlight.
